About me, e.g.d

I'm a 22 year old product designer from South London with lots of interests and hobbys. I love to surf, to box, to hike and I love to design.

The love of building and making things began with helping my dad from a young age, building garden summer houses, sheds and furniture. This grew my love of design and instilled in me the belief that I can make not just products I need but products others are in need of too. I studied product design at the University of Brighton. During my 4 years at university my design skills were constantly adapting and growing, each tutorial with lecturers and tasks lead me to how I design today, with my three principles at the core of each and every project I take on — sustainability, functionality and inclusivity.

I graduated in 2026 with a Bachelor of Science in Product Design, with a professional placement year.
